Hi, I'm Magaly

— but every player calls me Coach M

  • Licensed coach
  • 12+ years playing
  • 4 years coaching kids

I've loved soccer for as long as I can remember — playing it, watching it, studying it. Along the way I had amazing coaches who shaped me, and coaching is how I get to pass that on.

See the programs Book a lesson →

Soccer taught me things that reach far past the field. Determination and resilience aren't something you only pick up in a sport — they're life skills. This game is what made me disciplined, determined, and resilient, and that's what I want every player to carry with them long after the session ends.

My coaching style is built around one thing above all: confidence. We learn and master the basics until they're second nature, because real confidence comes from knowing you can do it. From there, everything else grows.

Every player is different, so I plan every session around the individual. We start with an evaluation, I build each practice around what that child needs, and I check in with parents for what they're seeing at home and at games.
